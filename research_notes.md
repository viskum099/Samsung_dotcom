# India Samsung mobile research notes

## Scope used

- Market: India, as requested.
- Time window: interpreted “last years data” as 2025–2026, through 7 November 2026. Some marketplace pages show review ages relatively rather than a full date; those are marked in the evidence notes.
- Product research CSV scope: the original scan covers Samsung mobile discussions and Galaxy A56 5G / Galaxy S25 Ultra examples where a source names the model. The static promotional page now demonstrates Galaxy S26 Ultra and Galaxy A56 5G at the user's request. Do not transfer A56 or S25 feedback to S26 Ultra. Galaxy S26 Ultra specifications shown on the page are sourced separately from its official Samsung India product pages.
- Source set requested: Reddit, Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, Amazon, and Flipkart. Publicly indexed Reddit, Flipkart, Amazon, and YouTube pages were found. The search pass did not surface verifiable product-specific Facebook or Instagram post/comment content; those are marked as coverage gaps.

## How to read the CSVs

- `india_mobile_research_findings.csv` is a small, purposive source sample, not a statistically representative survey. It includes both positive and critical signals and labels editorial video review separately from customer comments.
- `india_mobile_customer_profiles.csv` contains early hypotheses. “Willingness to pay” is reported only where a source has an explicit individual budget or paid amount. A person's budget, purchase price, rating, or complaint is not treated as a population estimate.
- `india_mobile_trend_spec_hook_drafts.csv` shows the mapping method: source signal → customer need → exact, cited specification → qualified hook draft. The example product specifications are from Samsung India product pages for Galaxy A56 5G. They must not be reused for another model.
- Aggregate ratings and visible likes/votes are contextual metadata, not independent respondent counts or sentiment measures.

## Collection limits

This work used public search-indexed pages; it did not scrape private, login-restricted, or non-indexed content and did not download entire review corpora. YouTube indexed video information was available, but comment-level coding was not. Facebook and Instagram content needs accessible public post URLs or an authorized export to include reliably. For a course study, retain links and collection dates, paraphrase rather than republish long user text, omit usernames, and follow platform terms and applicable privacy rules.
